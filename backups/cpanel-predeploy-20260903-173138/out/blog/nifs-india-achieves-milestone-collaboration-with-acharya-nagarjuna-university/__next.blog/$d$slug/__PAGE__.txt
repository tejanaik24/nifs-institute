<h3 data-start="74" data-end="157">NIFS India Achieves Milestone Collaboration with Acharya Nagarjuna University</h3>
<p data-start="159" data-end="544">In a significant development in the field of fire and safety education, <strong data-start="231" data-end="245">NIFS India</strong> (National Institute of Fire &amp; Safety) has established a prestigious academic collaboration with Acharya Nagarjuna University. This collaboration aims to enhance educational standards in the fire and safety domain, providing students with world-class training and industry-relevant certifications.</p>
<p data-start="546" data-end="1100"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" src="/images/blog/nifs-india-achieves-milestone-collaboration-with-acharya-nagarjuna-university-65.jpeg" alt="" width="1024" height="683"></p>
<p data-start="532" data-end="595" style="caret-color: rgb(0, 0, 0); color: rgb(0, 0, 0);">The MoU signing was graced by eminent dignitaries, including:</p>
<ul data-start="596" data-end="934" style="caret-color: rgb(0, 0, 0); color: rgb(0, 0, 0);">
<li data-start="596" data-end="671"><strong data-start="598" data-end="621">Dr. K Ganadhara Rao</strong>&nbsp;– Vice Chancellor, Acharya Nagarjuna University</li>
<li data-start="672" data-end="742"><strong data-start="674" data-end="698">Prof. G. Simhachalam</strong>&nbsp;– Registrar, Acharya Nagarjuna University</li>
<li data-start="743" data-end="811"><strong data-start="745" data-end="770">Smt. K Ratnashilamani</strong>&nbsp;– Rector, Acharya Nagarjuna University</li>
<li data-start="812" data-end="888"><strong data-start="814" data-end="840">Prof. V. Venkateswarlu</strong>&nbsp;– Director, CDE, Acharya Nagarjuna University</li>
<li data-start="889" data-end="934"><strong data-start="891" data-end="914">Sri. Suneel Mahanty</strong>&nbsp;– CEO, NIFS India</li><li data-start="889" data-end="934"><span style="font-family: -webkit-standard; font-size: medium;"><b>K Kushuma Kumari &#8211;</b>&nbsp;General Manager, Operations, NIFS India</span><br></li>
</ul>
<p data-start="546" data-end="1100">&nbsp;This collaboration marks a new era in professional training, bridging the gap between theoretical learning and practical implementation.</p>
<p data-start="546" data-end="1100"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" src="/images/blog/nifs-india-achieves-milestone-collaboration-with-acharya-nagarjuna-university-66.jpeg" alt="" width="534" height="487"></p>
<p data-start="1102" data-end="1455">Sri Suneel Mahanty, CEO of <strong data-start="1129" data-end="1143">NIFS India</strong>, expressed his gratitude and commitment to delivering top-notch education in fire safety, occupational health, and disaster management. He highlighted that this collaboration will facilitate cutting-edge research, modernized curricula, and skill development programs that align with global industry standards.</p>
<p data-start="1457" data-end="1856">The MoU signifies a joint commitment to promoting excellence in fire and safety training, ensuring that students gain hands-on experience and expertise in critical safety protocols. With this collaboration, Acharya Nagarjuna University and <strong data-start="1697" data-end="1711">NIFS India</strong> are set to produce highly skilled professionals who will contribute to workplace safety, risk management, and emergency response preparedness.</p>
<p data-start="1457" data-end="1856"><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" src="/images/blog/nifs-india-achieves-milestone-collaboration-with-acharya-nagarjuna-university-67.jpeg" alt="" width="768" height="870"></p>
<p data-start="1858" data-end="2127">This milestone is a testament to <strong data-start="1891" data-end="1905">NIFS India</strong>’s dedication to advancing fire and safety education in India. It opens new avenues for students, offering career-oriented courses that cater to the growing demand for fire safety professionals across various industries.</p>
<p data-start="2129" data-end="2325">This initiative has garnered widespread recognition, as featured in prominent Telugu news publications such as Sakshi and Eenadu, reinforcing its significance in the academic and safety sectors.</p>
<p data-start="2327" data-end="2434">For more updates on this collaboration and its benefits for students, stay connected with <strong data-start="2417" data-end="2431">NIFS India</strong>!</p>
