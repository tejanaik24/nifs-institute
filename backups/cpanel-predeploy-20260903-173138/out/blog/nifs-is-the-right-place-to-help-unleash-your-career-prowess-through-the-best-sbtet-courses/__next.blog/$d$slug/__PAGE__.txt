<p>The globalized world requires experts with advanced and certain area specific technical knowledge to grow in any field of work. The National Institute of Fire and Safety (NIFS) provides the One year Certificate Course in Industrial Safety affiliated with SBTET, Government of Andhra Pradesh. This course is aimed at giving the knowledge and skills needed by students to foster industrial safety and thereby prepare them for a successful career in the industries of their choice.</p>
<p><strong>What is SBTET?</strong></p>
<p>The State Board of Technical Education and Training (SBTET) is an important educational board of India which is operate and control the technical education system in the country. As an organisation that was set up to encourage technical education, SBTET offers many diploma, advanced diploma and certificate courses in response to the needs of the various sectors.</p>
<p>This all-encompassing one-year certificate course has now been announced by NIFS, which is a division of SSBIHS and recognized and affiliated with SBTET. The courses are carefully developed to meet the requirements of the profession, which makes it possible to describe the educational program as suitable for individuals who plan to work in the sphere of industrial safety. The course is also recognized by the Inspector of Factories and thus, the certification is also valid and worthwhile.</p>

</ul>
<p><strong>Course Duration</strong></p>

<p>Aconcise Syllabi and Courses of Study Responsive to Market Provisions</p>
<p>NIFS offers an SBTET-certified course that comprises numerous topics that are essential to industrial safety profession. <p><strong>Key subjects covered in the course include:</strong></p>
<ul>
