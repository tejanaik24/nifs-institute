<p><b>Course Overview</b></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Students at NIFS can complete the DHSE program for one year which delivers complete training in vital safety competencies. <p><span style="font-weight: 400;">During your course you will learn important subjects that include the following:</span></p>
<ol>
<li><b> Fire Engineering</b></li>
</ol>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Any organization depends on effective fire safety programs as their operational foundation. This subject teaches you:</span></p>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Fire prevention techniques</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Fire risk assessment and management</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Firefighting equipment and technologies</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Emergency response planning</span></li>
</ul>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The knowledge of fire behavioral patterns combined with suppression techniques will enable you to tackle fire-related risks throughout every workplace.</span></p>
<ol start="2">
<li><b> Industrial Safety</b></li>
</ol>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Industries come with inherent risks. In this subject, you’ll learn:</span></p>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Safety protocols for heavy machinery</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Hazard identification and control measures</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Safety legislation and compliance</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Accident investigation and reporting</span></li>
</ul>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The information plays an essential role in protecting personnel while meeting industrial regulatory standards.</span></p>
<ol start="3">
<li><b> Constructional Safety</b></li>
</ol>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Construction sites are high-risk zones. This module covers:</span></p>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Fall prevention and scaffolding safety</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Safe handling of construction materials</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Equipment and tool safety</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Site safety audits and inspections</span></li>
</ul>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The global construction industry surge increases demand for safety officers to lead effective safety protocols.</span></p>
<ol start="4">
<li><b> Occupational Health and Environment</b></li>
</ol>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Workplace health and the protection of the environment represent essential components for managing security within operations. You’ll explore:</span></p>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Workplace hazard analysis (chemical, biological, physical)</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Personal protective equipment (PPE)</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Health surveillance and wellness programs</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Environmental impact assessments</span></li>
</ul>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Students who learn this subject gain skills to design protective workplaces together with improved workplace wellness systems and lower environmental impairment.</span></p>
