<p>Emergency Action Planning: industrial safety courses demonstrate how to perform risk assessments, and then architect site-specific plans around evacuation routes, muster points, resource allocation, and more. Role-playing reinforces familiarity.</p>
<p>Fire Safety Fundamentals: Combining science lessons with interactive simulations, participants observe how various materials burn based on temperature and oxygen concentration. This informs tactical decision-making.</p>
