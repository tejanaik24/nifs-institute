<p><strong>What is safety Management : </strong></p>
<p>Safety management is commonly understood as applying a set of principles, framework, processes and measures to prevent accidents, injuries and other adverse consequences that may be caused by using a service or a product.</p>
<p>Safety management is an organizational function, which ensures that all safety risks have been identified, assessed and satisfactorily mitigated.</p>

<li><strong> Enhance Productivity: </strong>By Promoting a safer and healthier work environment, employee morale and motivation, directly contributing to increased productivity and efficiency. Employees feel more confident and engaged when they know their employers value their wellbeing.</li>
