<p><strong>Employment Sectors with Referee to Fire Safety</strong></p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<ol>
<li><strong> Fire Safety Inspection and Enforcement</strong></li>
</ol>
<ul>
<li><strong>Fire Inspector:</strong> This agency carries out assessments with a view of ascertaining levels of compliance with norms and regulations under the docket of Fire Safety.</li>
<li><strong>Fire Marshal:</strong> Is responsible for implementing and the prevention of fire laws and delves into fire related cases.</li>
<li><strong>Code Enforcement Officer:</strong> Performs the task of checking that everyone follows the fire safety standards.</li>
</ul>
<ol start="2">
<li><strong> Fire Protection Engineering</strong></li>
</ol>
<ul>
<li><strong>Fire Protection Engineer: </strong>Develops fire fighting systems such as sprinkler system and fire alarm system.</li>
<li><strong>Fire Safety Engineer:</strong> Performs risk analyses and develops plans of fire protection.</li>
<li><strong>Fire System Designer:</strong> Specialises in the design and installation of fire fighting and alarm systems.</li>
</ul>
<ol start="3">
<li><strong> Fire Safety Consulting</strong></li>
</ol>
<ul>
<li><strong>Fire Safety Consultant: </strong>Offers professional guidance to various measures of preventing fire incidences.</li>
<li><strong>Fire Risk Assessor:</strong> In other words, risk assessments are used to identify the fire hazards.</li>
<li><strong>Fire Safety Auditor:</strong> Inspects premises to check compliance with provisions of the fire safety law.</li>
</ul>
<ol start="4">
<li><strong> Fire Service and Operations</strong></li>
</ol>
<ul>
<li><strong>Firefighter:</strong> Avails itself in times of disasters and puts out fire.</li>
<li><strong>Fire Officer:</strong> Supervises firefighters during the operations in emergency incidences.</li>
<li><strong>Emergency Medical Technician (EMT): </strong>Offers medical support in case of an emergency.</li>
</ul>
<ol start="5">
<li><strong> Fire Safety Training and Education</strong></li>
</ol>
<ul>
<li><strong>Fire Safety Trainer:</strong> Provides training regarding fire safety.</li>
<li><strong>Fire Safety Educator: </strong>Promote the information about how to avoid fire and prevent it from happening.</li>
<li><strong>Fire Safety Instructor:</strong> Currently, the author has been teaching courses in fire safety measures.</li>
</ul>
<ol start="6">
<li><strong> Fire Safety, Research and Development</strong></li>
</ol>
<ul>
<li><strong>Fire Safety Researcher: </strong>Designs unique fire safety products and services.</li>
<li><strong>Fire Safety Scientist: </strong>Develops high-end fire protection systems solutions.</li>
<li><strong>R&amp;D Fire Safety Engineer: </strong>Develops new fire protection schemes and experiments new fire fighting methods.</li>
</ul>
<ol start="7">
<li><strong> Other Fire Safety Roles</strong></li>
</ol>
<ul>
<li><strong>Fire Safety Manager: </strong>Responsible for fire safety in organizations or institutions.</li>
<li><strong>Fire Safety Coordinator: Supervises fire safety work and obligation.</strong></li>
<li><strong>Fire Safety Specialist:</strong> Responsible for bringing the knowledge about the fire regulations and safety measures.</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Essentials of Fire Certificates and Qualifications</strong></p>
<p><strong>Educational Qualifications</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Diploma holders in Fire Safety Engineering from National Institute of Fire and Safety (NIFS), Visakhapatnam etc.</li>
<li>National Diploma in Fire Science (e.g., from NFSC or IIFE) or equivalent Bachelor’s Degree in Fire Engineering.</li>
<li>Acoountants with Bachelor’s Degree in Safety and Fire Engineering (For example, from JNTU).</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Professional Certifications</strong></p>
<ul>

<li>Certified Fire Protection Specialist (CFPS) by National Fire Protection Association Indian Section.</li>
<li>Listed Certified Fire Inspector (CFI) by the International Code Council (ICC) India.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Specialized Training Programs</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Fire Safety Training by NFSC.</li>
<li>Emergency Response and Disaster Management Training by NIDM.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Government Certifications</strong></p>
