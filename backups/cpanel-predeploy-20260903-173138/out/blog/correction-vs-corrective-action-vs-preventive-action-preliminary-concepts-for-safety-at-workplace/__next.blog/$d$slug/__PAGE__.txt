e:T313c,<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Health and safety are an essential aspect of any organization, and control of risks as well as nonconformities are core competencies in any workplace. Three concepts play vital roles in this regard, The essential categories that a quality management system has to contain are: <strong>Correction, Corrective Action and Preventive Action</strong>. Familiarity with the differences between these terms and usage of them in practice can considerably help improve your organization’s security and productivity.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>What is Correction?</strong></p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Correction therefore entails trying to fix a problem or defect in a manner that reduces its effects on a particular system. It is a crisis sensitive mechanism which is employed before, during or after conflict has occurred.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Key Features of Correction:</strong></p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Immediate Action:</strong> Most of the time it fails to deal with the real issue and provides a short-term solution to a complicated issue.</li>



<li><strong>Problem Fixing:</strong> It is also designed to prevent the nonconformity from progressing causing more loss or affecting other operations in the organization.</li>



<li><strong>Temporary Solution:</strong> It is mostly temporary to address problems because it truly works for exceptions and not for solutions.</li>
</ul>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Example:</strong> Suppose there is a fire outbreak in the workplace. To demonstrate a correction, the person uses water or a fire extinguisher on the fire. The first problem disappears while the second – the cause of fire – still persists.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>What is Corrective Action?</strong></p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Preventive action entails finding out why a problem occurred so that such a problem will not persist in the same or a similar way. This is a systematic process, which is carried after the identification of a particular nonconformity.</p>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Key Features of Corrective Action:</strong></p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Root Cause Analysis: </strong>It goes further trying to determine the root course of the issue and help to eradicate it.</li>



<li><strong>Prevention of Recurrence:</strong> They are used to keep such incidents from recurring because they aim at correcting problems that have been made.</li>



<li><strong>Systematic Approach:</strong> It is comprehensive in its approach and especially entails screening, designing, and checking viability.</li>
</ul>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Example:</strong> After a fire incident in the organization, the body analyses the situation and determines the cause of the fire. In the case where it would be decided that flammable materials were stored improperly, the mitigation action would entail putting in measures to safely store such material, and educating employee to adhere to the measures.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>What is Preventive Action?</strong></p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Preventive action means taking steps that involve finding possible problems and minimizing the possibility of that problem arising. It is a preventive strategy that seeks to avoid issues arising in the first place.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Key Features of Preventive Action:</strong></p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Proactive Measures:</strong> It is proactive in managing risk situations before they become issues known to its target audiences.</li>



<li><strong>Risk Identification: </strong>Is primarily centred on assessment of risks and threats in activities or structures.</li>



<li><strong>Long-Term Focus:</strong> Preventive measures have further goals and objectives to strive for and to ensure permanent safety control.</li>
</ul>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Example: </strong>This way the organization might organize risk assessments to avoid fires, and make sure that the flammable materials are not accumulated in zones which have increased danger and that all procedures concerning fire protection are maintained appropriately.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Key Differences</strong></h3>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>HSE (Health, Safety, and Environment) Applications.</strong></p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>1. Correction:</strong></p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Handling of spill or leak situations before it can lead to an injury.</li>



<li>Administering care after an incurring an injury is another form of first aid.</li>



<li>Substituting a worn out defective spare part that has caused a breakdown while the machine was in use.</li>
</ul>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>2. Corrective Action:</strong></p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>To find out why equipment often breaks down and coming up with the best schedule for maintenance.</li>



<li>Such tools as the root cause analysis to define why near-misses happened and alter conditions responsible for failure occurrences.</li>
</ul>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>3. Preventive Action:</strong></p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>He or she has to organise safety drills and training.</li>



<li>Risk assessment and management should be instituted and integrated within the business operations more particularly in;</li>



<li>Check and follow safety standard through the conduct of audit.</li>
</ul>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">It is possible to revolutionise how workplaces tackle and prevent workplace safety issues by using Correction, Corrective Action, and Preventive Action. As correction focuses on solving problems at the current level of organization, corrective measures go further exposing the possible causes for similar problems to happen again while preventive measures rely on identification of possible risk factors beforehand. Taken altogether, those efforts provide a strong foundation for protection of the workforce and improvement of operational activities. In an organization and society at NIFS our major focus is creating awareness of relative ideas and enhancing relevant knowledge to enable change in our work setting.</p>
