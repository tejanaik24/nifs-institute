<h2>Fire Safety Regulations and Certifications</h2>
<h3>National and International Standards</h3>
<p>At NIFS, we place utmost importance on adhering to national and international fire safety standards. Currently, there are diverse approaches and requirements across the world regarding fire safety, leading to significant variations in building design, construction methods, product approvals, and operational practices . To address this fragmentation, the International Fire Safety Standards Common Principles (IFSS-CP) have been developed to provide a connected and consistent approach .</p>
<p>The IFSS-CP establishes overarching, performance-based common principles for fire safety engineering design, construction, occupation, and ongoing management . It is intended to be flexible and non-prescriptive, allowing for incremental adoption and promoting good practices . These Common Principles are universally applicable, regardless of existing codes, standards, and guidance already in place .</p>
<p>The IFSS-CP framework articulates the functions, life span characteristics, and attributes of fire safety in buildings. It can be used to determine general and specific functions, hierarchies, competencies, knowledge, and skills required for effective fire safety management. Compliance with IFSS-CP necessitates disclosing all information sources compiled, including their creation date, provenance, original purpose, compilation method, limitations, assumptions, and adequacy.</p>
<ol>
<li>The IFSS-CP is based on five key principles :</li>
</ol>
<ul>
<li>Prevention of fire ignition</li>
<li>Limiting fire spread</li>
<li>Structural integrity and fire resistance</li>
<li>Evacuation and firefighting</li>
<li>Resilience and recovery</li>
</ul>
<ol start="3">
<li>The adoption of IFSS-CP aims to :</li>
</ol>
<ul>
<li>Improve consistency in fire safety practices</li>
<li>Facilitate collaboration among professionals</li>
<li>Enhance public safety and confidence</li>
<li>Promote best practices and innovation</li>
</ul>
<p>By embracing the IFSS-CP, we strive to align our fire and safety courses with globally recognised standards, ensuring our graduates are well-versed in the latest principles and practices.</p>
