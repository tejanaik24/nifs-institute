<ol>
<li>Fire Safety Manager: As a fire safety manager, you will be responsible for ensuring the safety of a building or facility by identifying potential fire hazards, conducting risk assessments, and implementing preventive measures. <li>Fire Safety Consultant: In this role, you will provide expert advice and consultancy services to businesses, organisations, and government agencies on fire safety best practices. You will conduct assessments, audits, and provide recommendations to<br />

the public on fire safety.</li>
<li>Fire Inspector: As a fire inspector, your role will involve inspecting buildings and facilities for compliance with fire safety regulations. You will identify potential fire hazards, ensure the proper functioning of fire safety equipment, and enforce fire<br />
codes and regulations.</li>
