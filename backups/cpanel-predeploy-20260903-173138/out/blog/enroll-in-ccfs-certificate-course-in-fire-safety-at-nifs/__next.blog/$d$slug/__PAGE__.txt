<h4><b>Course Structure: What You Will Learn</b></h4>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">It is important to know everything from the ground up, in a structured manner but at the same time, you do not want to be focused on something too broad while at the same time being a novice at something specific.</span></p>
<ul>
<li aria-level="1"><b>Paper 1: Fundamentals of Fire and Safety.</b></li>
</ul>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">This paper aims at establishing the fundamental concepts of fire prevention and protection. You will learn about basic fire chemistry, the way of classifying them and the type of extinguishers used to put out different types of fires. Also, students will be able to discover what can be done to prevent fires, how to protect oneself during a fire, and learn about risk assessments.</span></li>
</ul>
<ul>
<li aria-level="1"><b>Paper 2: Safety in Construction</b></li>
</ul>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">This segment of the construction business is dangerous and involves many risks including falling, mechanical failures among others. In this paper, you will learn more about safety aspects that should be taken into consideration on the construction sites. These are areas like PPE, hazards, and safety at work and adherence to safety laws.</span></li>
</ul>
<ul>
<li aria-level="1"><b>Paper 3: Disaster Management and Toxication Discharge</b></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">This paper is aimed at making you ready in case of any emergency. Understand emergency planning, the use of the Disaster Relief Symphony and handling with dangerous substances. Moreover, civil security involves issues to do with environment pollution including its sources, consequences and ways of preventing it from affecting the health of the population.</span></li>
</ul>
