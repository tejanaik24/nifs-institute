<h2>Detailed Breakdown of Top 10 Fire and Safety Courses</h2>

<h3>1. Diploma in Fire & Safety (DFS) — Most Popular After 12th</h3>
<p>The <strong>Diploma in Fire & Safety (DFS)</strong> is a 1-year comprehensive course designed for students who have passed 12th standard. It covers fire prevention science, fire-fighting hydraulics, hazard identification, and industrial rescue operations.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Eligibility:</strong> 10+2 Pass in any stream (Science, Commerce, or Arts).</li>
<li><strong>Career Outcome:</strong> Safety Officer, Fire Safety Assistant, Site Safety Supervisor.</li>
<li><strong>Starting Salary:</strong> ₹22,000 – ₹35,000 per month in India.</li>
</ul>

<h3>2. Advanced Diploma in Industrial Safety (ADIS) — High Industry Demand</h3>
<p>ADIS is the gold standard for statutory safety officer appointments under the Factories Act 1948. It focuses heavily on hazard risk assessment, chemical safety, machine guarding, and occupational health.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Eligibility:</strong> B.Sc (Physics/Chemistry), B.E., B.Tech, or 3-year Polytechnic Diploma.</li>
<li><strong>Career Outcome:</strong> Statutory Safety Officer, HSE Manager, Safety Consultant.</li>
<li><strong>Starting Salary:</strong> ₹35,000 – ₹60,000 per month.</li>
</ul>

<h3>3. PG Diploma in Health, Safety & Environment (PG DHSE)</h3>
<p>Post Graduate Diploma in HSE bridges industrial safety management with environmental sustainability. Graduates are trained in ISO 14001, OHSAS 18001/ISO 45001 auditing, and waste management.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Eligibility:</strong> Graduation in Science or Engineering stream.</li>
<li><strong>Career Outcome:</strong> EHS Specialist, Environmental Compliance Auditor.</li>
<li><strong>Starting Salary:</strong> ₹38,000 – ₹65,000 per month.</li>
</ul>

<h3>4. B.Sc in Fire & Industrial Safety — 3-Year Degree Program</h3>
<p>For students seeking a formal university degree after 12th Science, the B.Sc in Fire & Industrial Safety offers an in-depth academic foundation combined with practical industrial visits and live fire drill simulations.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Eligibility:</strong> 12th Science (Physics, Chemistry, Math/Biology).</li>
<li><strong>Career Outcome:</strong> Safety Engineer, Assistant Safety Director, PSU Safety Cadre.</li>
<li><strong>Starting Salary:</strong> ₹30,000 – ₹50,000 per month.</li>
</ul>
