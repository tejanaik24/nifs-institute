f:T2a28,<section class="wpb-content-wrapper"><p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The protection of workers represents the number one priority for industries around the world today. The environment of every construction project and factory workspace and every industrial facility must be maintained free from risk through the work of safety professionals. Students who want to establish a career in health and safety should choose the PG DHSE at NIFS (National Institute of Fire and Safety Management).</span></p>
<p><b>Why Choose PG DHSE?</b></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The PG Diploma in Health Safety &amp; Environment provides complete program instruction which teaches fundamental workplace safety skills and practical knowledge to students. You can get ready for industrial work after completing this program which provides instruction in fire safety alongside industrial risks and environmental safety.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">This essay examines both the curriculum contents and career benefits of this program.</span></p>
<p><b><img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="wp-image-6867 alignleft" src="/images/blog/how-trained-safety-technicians-in-vizag-are-transforming-workplace-safety-standards-13.jpg" alt="PG Diploma in Health, Safety &amp; Environment" width="517" height="344" srcset="https://www.nifsindia.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/18488106-300x200.jpg 300w, https://www.nifsindia.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/18488106-1024x682.jpg 1024w, https://www.nifsindia.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/18488106-768x512.jpg 768w, https://www.nifsindia.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/18488106-1536x1024.jpg 1536w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 517px) 100vw, 517px" />Course Overview</b></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Students who want classroom learning will find the PG DHSE diploma course suitable since it operates through traditional in-person delivery during one academic year. The educational content matches the requirements established by industry and it includes protection standards for different workplace environments.</span></p>
<h3><b>Subjects Covered in PG DHSE</b></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The curriculum includes four major subjects:</span></p>
<ol>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Fire Engineering</b>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="2"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Basics of Fire Safety</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="2"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Fire Prevention &amp; Protection</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="2"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Fire Extinguishers and Suppression Systems</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="2"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Firefighting Equipment and Techniques</span></li>
</ul>
</li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Industrial Safety</b>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="2"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Hazard Identification and Risk Assessment (HIRA)</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="2"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Safety Regulations and Standards</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="2"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Workplace Safety Measures</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="2"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Handling of Hazardous Materials</span></li>
</ul>
</li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Constructional Safety</b>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="2"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Safety in Building and Construction</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="2"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="2"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Scaffolding Safety</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="2"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Excavation and Demolition Safety</span></li>
</ul>
</li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><b>Occupational Health and Environment</b>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="2"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Workplace Ergonomics</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="2"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Industrial Hygiene</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="2"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Environmental Management</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="2"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Pollution Control Measures</span></li>
</ul>
</li>
</ol>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Each of these subjects is designed to give students a </span><b>practical understanding of workplace safety</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> and prepare them for real-world challenges.</span></p>
<h3><b>Who Can Apply?</b></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The </span><b>eligibility criteria</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> for this course are simple. Anyone who has a </span><b>degree in Science, Technology, or Engineering</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> or has completed a </span><b>3-year diploma</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> can apply. This makes it an excellent option for graduates looking to specialize in safety management.</span></p>
