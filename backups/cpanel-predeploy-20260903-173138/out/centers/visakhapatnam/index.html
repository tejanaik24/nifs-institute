Built for Visakhapatnam’s Industries

Fire and safety officers aren’t optional hires in this city — Visakhapatnam’s largest employers are legally required to staff them.

Visakhapatnam Steel Plant (RINL)

~7.3 MTPA liquid steel capacity, ~28,000 employees at the Gajuwaka works.

HPCL Visakhapatnam Refinery

The first oil refinery on India's east coast — hydrocarbon processing with zero-margin fire response times.

Visakhapatnam Port

Major cargo port with strict onboard and yard fire-safety protocols.

Hindustan Shipyard Limited

Shipbuilding and Eastern Naval Command dockyard operations.

Jawaharlal Nehru Pharma City (JNPC)

Dedicated pharma SEZ near Parawada with multiple manufacturing units.

Courses Available at NIFS Visakhapatnam

Diploma · 1 Year

Diploma in Fire & Safety (DFS)

Builds working knowledge of fire engineering and site safety protocols, preparing graduates for supervisory-track roles on industrial and construction sites.

Eligibility: 10+2 / ITI / Any Stream

Advanced Diploma · 12 Months

Advanced Diploma in Fire & Safety (ADFS)

An in-depth program combining fire engineering with hands-on drills at NIFS's practical training yard, built for candidates targeting mid-level safety leadership roles.

Eligibility: 10+2 / Diploma (Any Stream)

Advanced Diploma · 12 Months

Advanced Diploma in Industrial Safety (ADIS)

Focused on plant-floor safety management — hazard control, machine safeguarding, and safety audits — for candidates aiming at core industrial safety officer roles.

Eligibility: 10+2 / Diploma (Any Stream)

Careers After Fire Safety Training in Visakhapatnam

Graduates go on to roles including Fire Warden, Site Safety Officer, Industrial Safety Officer, Plant Safety Coordinator, and Fire & Safety Manager. NIFS graduates specifically have been placed with employers including Adani, L&T, ITC, and Amazon.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is NIFS located in Visakhapatnam?

NIFS's headquarters is at Door No. 47-10-15, 2nd Lane, Dwarakanagar, AG Avenue Building, 3rd Floor, Visakhapatnam (A.P.) – 530016.

Which fire safety course should I start with in Visakhapatnam?

If you're starting fresh after 10th or 12th, the Diploma in Fire & Safety (DFS) is the standard entry point. If you already have a diploma and want a supervisory or industrial-safety track, the Advanced Diploma in Fire & Safety (ADFS) or Advanced Diploma in Industrial Safety (ADIS) is the next step.

Do NIFS Visakhapatnam courses include practical training?

Yes — NIFS runs a dedicated practical training yard for live equipment handling, drill scenarios, and hazard simulations rather than classroom theory alone.

Is NIFS Visakhapatnam government recognized?

Yes. NIFS is NSDC and Skill India approved, ISO 9001:2015 certified, with SBTET Andhra Pradesh recognition for diploma-level courses and degree affiliation through Acharya Nagarjuna University and Annamalai University.

Which industries in Visakhapatnam hire NIFS graduates?

Steel (Visakhapatnam Steel Plant / RINL), petroleum refining (HPCL Visakhapatnam Refinery), port and shipping operations, and pharmaceutical manufacturing (JNPC) are the largest local employers of certified fire and safety officers.
