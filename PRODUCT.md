## Anti-references

No visual/design anti-reference sites are specified in brand-voice.md — flag this gap to the client before any bolder/departure-mode design work. What IS specified are hard content bans (brand-voice.md "## Never use"), which anchor the Do's and Don'ts a DESIGN.md would later cite:
- Never claim "best institute in India," "#1 safety institute," "leading," or "world-class" without evidence.
- Never invent fee numbers — always "contact for fees."
- Never present NEBOSH/IOSH/OSHA as NIFS-taught courses (referenced for comparison only).
- Never use "comprehensive" as filler.

Competitively, NIFS should not read as generic or template-driven relative to icfsindia.com, greenwgroup.co.in, or redhatsafety.com — but no specific visual critique of those sites is on file.

## Design Principles

- **Evidence over hype.** Every claim is backed by a verifiable number or credential (NSDC, Skill India, ISO 9001:2015, SBTET, named employers). No unverifiable superlatives.
- **Credentials-first trust signals.** Accreditation and certification marks are prominent, not buried — they are the primary differentiator from competitors.
- **No invented figures.** Fees, rankings, and stats are never fabricated. Use "contact for fees" where real numbers aren't confirmed.
- **Career-outcome framing.** Content and CTAs orient toward placement/career outcomes (job roles, employers, salary context), not just course features.
