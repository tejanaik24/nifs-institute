### 1. Course Hub Pages (High Commercial Intent)
- **Primary Keywords:** `diploma in fire and safety`, `advanced diploma in industrial safety adis`, `pg diploma in health safety environment`, `bsc in fire safety`
- **Content Requirement:** Course duration, eligibility criteria (10th/12th/Graduate), syllabus breakdown, job roles unlocked, fee structure transparency, enrollment CTA.

### 2. City & Regional Hub Pages (Geo-Targeted Local SEO)
- **Primary Keywords:** `fire safety course in visakhapatnam`, `fire safety institute in hyderabad`, `industrial safety course in vijayawada`, `safety officer course near me`
- **Content Requirement:** Local center address, contact numbers, direct directions, regional batch timings, local recruiter tie-ups.

### 3. Career & Salary Guide Hub (Informational Intent & Top-of-Funnel)
- **Primary Keywords:** `safety officer salary in India`, `how to become a fire safety officer`, `gulf safety officer jobs salary`, `fire safety engineer scope`
- **Content Requirement:** Comprehensive career roadmap articles, salary comparison tables (India vs Gulf), industry demand breakdown (Oil & Gas, Construction, Manufacturing).

### 4. Accreditation & Government Approval Pages (E-E-A-T Boosters)
- **Primary Keywords:** `NSDC approved fire safety course`, `government recognized safety diploma`, `Skill India safety training`
- **Content Requirement:** Certificate verification process, approval letters/badge displays, university affiliations (Acharya Nagarjuna University - ANU).

## 🤖 Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) Strategy

To ensure dominance in Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, and Claude:

1. **Direct Answer Paragraphs:** Every course page will lead with a concise 40-50 word summary block answering:
   - What is the course?
   - Who is eligible?
   - What is the duration & career outcome?

2. **`llms.txt` Implementation:** Expose structured Markdown representation of all courses, credentials, and center locations at `/llms.txt`.

3. **Comparison Tables:** Structured HTML tables comparing course duration, eligibility, and target roles for easy LLM table extraction.

## 📌 Local SEO & Map Pack Playbook
